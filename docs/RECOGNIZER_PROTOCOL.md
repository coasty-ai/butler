# The recognizer protocol: what the voice helper says and hears

The `recognizer` port (`.data/design/modules.md` §2) is a protocol, not a tool: the voice helper `native/bin/coarena-voice` (built from `native/macos/Voice.swift`, `Speaker.swift`, `TurnPolicy.swift`, `WakePolicy.swift`) speaks newline-delimited JSON on its stdin and stdout, and Electron (`electron/voice.ts` `NativeVoice`, over `electron/controller.ts` `HelperProcess`) speaks it back. A replacement command is spawned in its place and must say the same things at the same moments. This document is derived from the source as it stands at HEAD; where the app tolerates a field's absence that is said, and where something is not yet pluggable that is said too (§7).

## 1. The process

- Spawned with no arguments: `spawn(binary, [], { stdio: "pipe" })`. Its environment is Electron's own. Two variables are the helper's private switches for local trials, never set by the packaged app: `BUTLER_TRACE_STANDBY` (`1`, or `text`) turns on `standby_trace` events; `BUTLER_FULL_DUPLEX=1` asks for echo cancellation on the input node and reports it as `voice_processing`.
- Exits on its own when stdin closes (a 2 s hard deadline) and when its parent pid changes (polled every 100 ms). Electron never sends a quit.
- Restarted in place when it exits: backoff 0.5, 1, 2 s, at most 5 restarts in 60 s, then "Voice helper is unavailable." Every request has a deadline (5 s; 120 s for `requestPermissions`); a request past it kills the helper (`SIGKILL`) and the restart follows. After a restart Electron sends `enable` and `configure` again with the whole configuration.
- stderr is drained and never read.
- Audio and text stay in the process: nothing is written to disk, and in standby nothing at all is written to stdout (no text, no levels).

## 2. Framing

Requests are one JSON object per line on stdin: `{"id": "<uuid>", "method": "<name>", ...fields}`, the fields flattened beside `method` (`HelperProcess.send` writes `JSON.stringify({ id, method, ...data })`). A line that is not a JSON object is skipped.

Replies are one JSON object per line on stdout: `{"id": <the request's id>, "result": {...}}` or `{"id": ..., "error": "<sentence>"}`. An unknown method answers `{"id", "error": "Unknown voice method."}`. Events are `{"event": "<name>", ...fields}`; Electron routes any line with a string `event` as an event and matches the rest by `id`. Replies and events are written from one serial queue; when stdout backs up (more than 20 lines pending) `audio_level` is dropped first and nothing else ever is.

## 3. Modes

`standby` (waiting for the wake phrase: nothing is emitted), `followUp` (a short window after a reply or a turn, behaving like standby until speech is detected), `handsFree` (a turn opened by the wake phrase or a window) and `pushToTalk` (a turn held on Option+Space). Only an activated turn (`handsFree`, `pushToTalk`) emits text and levels.

## 4. Events

Types: `s` string, `n` number, `i` integer, `b` boolean. A field marked `?` may be absent.

### The turn's text

| Event                    | Fields                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Sent when                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `recognition_update`     | `textLength: i`, `source: s` (`wake_prefixed_segment`, `command_segment`)                                                                                                                                                                   | The merged text changed; written right before `transcript_partial` as its content-free companion for the diagnostics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `transcript_partial`     | `text: s`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | The whole text of the turn so far (never a delta): the recognizer's segments merged (`TurnPolicy.swift` `absorbPartial`, `requestSegments`), the wake phrase and its misheard echoes stripped from the first segment, a later segment that opens with the wake phrase restarting the request. Sent on every change; never in standby or a window. Electron feeds it to the pill, the early start, the clause stream and the speculation. |
| `recognition_final`      | `textLength: i`, `source: s`                                                                                                                                                                                                                | The recognizer finalized one segment (content-free).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

| `transcript_final`       | `text: s`, `confidence: n` (0–1), `segments: i`                                                                                                                                                                                             | The turn's text, every segment finalized by the recognizer; `confidence` is the word-weighted mean of the segments' confidences. | `voice_error`            | `message: s`, `code: s` (`empty`, `unfinalized`, `mic`, `permission`, `asleep`, `unavailable`, `secure_input`)                                                                                                                              | An activated turn ended without text: nothing heard (`empty`), recognizer errors over more than three rotations (`unfinalized`), the microphone (`mic`), no grant (`permission`), the Mac asleep (`asleep`), no on-device recognizer for the locale (`unavailable`), a password field took the keyboard during a hands-free turn (`secure_input`). `message` is the sentence the pill shows.                                             |
| `voice_cancelled`        |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Escape during a held key or an activated turn; or sleep or a session change during a command turn. Electron stops the run ("Stopped.").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `audio_level`            | `level: n` (0–1)                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | About every 80 ms during an activated turn only (RMS × 12, capped). The first line dropped under back-pressure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

Exactly one outcome follows every activation: `transcript_final`, `transcript_recovered`, `transcript_unconfirmed`, `voice_error` or `voice_cancelled`. `scripts/endpoint-report.mjs` and `scripts/streaming-report.mjs` pair activations with outcomes on that rule.

## 5. Commands

Every command answers `result` (or `error`). The fields are read leniently: a wrong type is ignored, not refused, except where a reply says `invalid`.

## 6. What a replacement command must do

- **Own its microphone and speech permission.** The grants belong to the binary that opens the microphone; the helper's are its own (`Voice-Info.plist`), and a replacement needs its own bundle identifier and usage strings. It answers `status` with `microphone` and `speech` truthfully and `requestPermissions` by asking macOS itself.
- **Emit the same events with the same timing meaning.** An activation event first; every `transcript_partial` the whole merged text so far, with the wake phrase stripped, sent as soon as the text changes (the clause stream commits on the words' arrival time and re-pushes the last partial itself after 350 ms of silence, so a recognizer that batches words commits later); `endpoint_near` about 0.7 s before the endpoint when it can; `shortcut_up` then `turn_endpoint`; then exactly one outcome. `transcript_final` only for text the recognizer finalized, with a real `confidence`; anything less is `transcript_recovered` at confidence 0 or `transcript_unconfirmed`, since the approval of a pending action rides on that distinction (`src/voice/router.ts` `voiceCommandConfidence`).
- **Latch input at every activation.** The helper sends `SIGUSR1` to the pid given as `controllerPID`, after checking that the pid's executable is named `coarena-controller` (`validControllerPath`), so the controller stops acting the moment the person speaks. Electron does not do this itself ("The helper already latched input" in `electron/main.ts`); a replacement that skips it lets a run keep clicking while the person talks.
- **Keep content local.** Before activation nothing about the room may leave the process. The partials and the final go to Electron over the private pipe only; the diagnostics record `textLength` (the text itself only in verbose mode). A command that sends audio or text to a service must say so in its own description; the privacy tier (`PRIVATE_LOCAL`) is not enforced on the recognizer command by the app today, which cannot see where a child process connects, so the Settings › Modules row for the recognizer should say as much when a command is chosen.
- **Answer every command**, if only honestly: `enable` with `enabled: false` when there is no event tap (push-to-talk is then unavailable and the wake phrase is the only way in), `speak` and `playPcmStart` with `accepted: false, reason: "disabled"` when it does not play audio (replies then stay on the pill; `electron/speech-output.ts` treats a refusal as a reply that did not play), `listen` with `opened: false, reason: "disabled"` when it keeps no windows (conversation mode then degrades to one turn per wake phrase, and Electron never waits for a window it was refused).
- **Exit when stdin closes** and survive being restarted with `configure` sent again.

## 7. Not yet pluggable

Said plainly: the `recognizer` port swaps the whole binary, and the binary does more than recognize.

- **The wake gate's phrase set lives in the helper.** The name's spellings (`WakePolicy.swift` `wakeNamePattern`), the optional "Hey", and the gate that lets the name count only before a pause, a task verb, a control or reply word or a question opener (`wakeOpeners`, `wakeControlWords`, `wakeReplyWords`, `commandAfterWakePhrase`) are Swift constants, mirrored in `src/voice/turns.ts` and pinned by `tests/fixtures/voice-phrases.json`. A replacement brings its own wake phrase; the app has no way to hand it one.
- **The endpoint timing** (`TurnPolicy.swift` `endpointTiming`: the stable/quiet pairs by completeness and patience, 45 s maximum, the 1.8 s final deadline) and the completeness rules are the helper's; `patience` is the one knob `configure` passes.
- **Push-to-talk** (the Option+Space event tap, `shortcut_*`), **spoken replies and PCM playback** (the `speak` and `playPcm*` family, the earcons) and **follow-up windows** are in the same process as recognition. A replacement recognizer that does not implement them loses them; there is no separate port for the speaker or the shortcut yet (`tts` in the design covers the sentence, not the playback path through this helper).
- **The vocabulary** reaches the recognizer as Apple's `contextualStrings`; a replacement receives the same list in `configure` and does with it what it can.
- **The stop latch** is a signal to a named sibling process, not a message on this protocol.

## 8. What this document could not settle from the source

Which fields a replacement may omit is inferred from what `electron/main.ts`, `electron/conversation.ts` and `src/voice/router.ts` read, not from a schema: `VoiceEvent` in `electron/voice.ts` declares every field optional. `listening_ready`, `recognition_update` and `recognition_final` reach the diagnostics only. The exact moment `endpoint_near` should fire relative to a replacement's own endpoint is a choice the replacement makes; the app only needs it before `turn_endpoint`.
